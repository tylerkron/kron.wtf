I'm Tyler. I've been building software for a long time, mostly the unglamorous layer underneath: integrations, platforms, and the systems that keep everything else running. These days that layer is for agents. Memory, permissions, tool access, and figuring out what happens when something goes sideways at 2am.

Proud husband and dad first. Engineer second. I co-founded DAQiFi and Our Trove, I wrote a short book on contract testing, and sometimes build nerdy things in the home lab. I live in Colorado and spend too much of my time thinking about the Broncos.
