I'm Tyler. I care about what makes agents useful in practice: memory, permissions, tool access, and figuring out what happens when something goes sideways at 2am.

I have an entrepreneurial streak. I co-founded [DAQiFi]({{< ref "products/daqifi" >}}) and [Our Trove]({{< ref "products/our-trove" >}}), and I like taking an idea from "someone should build this" to something people can use. My default is to make the first move, test the idea, and keep building.

Proud husband and dad first. Engineer second. I also co-authored a short book on contract testing and sometimes build nerdy things in the home lab. I live in Colorado and spend too much of my time thinking about the Broncos.
